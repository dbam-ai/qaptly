## Installation

### First-time Installation
Download `Qaptly-0.14.2-arm64.dmg` from the releases page and install.

### Updating from v0.14.x
The app will automatically update to v0.14.2 when you restart it.

### Manual Update
Download `Qaptly-0.14.2-arm64-mac.zip`, extract, and replace your existing app.
