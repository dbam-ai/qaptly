# Qaptly v0.17.1

## What's New

### Subscription Management
- View and manage your subscription plan directly in the app
- See your current plan details, credits balance, and billing period
- Upgrade, downgrade, or purchase additional credits without leaving the app

### Improved Test Execution Stability
- Enhanced recovery when test steps encounter unexpected situations during automated execution
- Toggle recovery behavior on or off in Settings → General → Execution

### Two-Factor Authentication Setup (Now in Desktop App)
- Previously, setting up two-factor authentication required the web console — the desktop app only supported verification at login
- You can now complete the full 2FA setup directly in the app, including authenticator app and email verification
- Manage recovery codes without switching to the browser

### State-Based Planning (AI Assistant)
- New "State Mode" in the AI Assistant for goal-based test planning
- Describe your desired start and end states in plain language
- The system automatically finds a path through your recorded scenes to reach the goal

## Fixes & Improvements

- Fixed an issue where the AI assistant connection was not detected when launching the app from Finder or Dock
- Improved navigation for sidebars with many items
- Clearer error messages for network connection issues
- Cleaned up the login screen
