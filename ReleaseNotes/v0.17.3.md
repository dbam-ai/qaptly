# Qaptly v0.17.3

## What's New

### App Learning & Memory
Qaptly can now learn and remember the screens in your app across recording sessions. After you record interactions, the AI automatically analyzes the screens, names them, and stores the knowledge for future test runs. This helps Qaptly understand where it is in your app and make smarter decisions during automated testing.

### Smarter AI Test Execution
- The AI assistant now reasons step-by-step through each action, verifying the result before moving on — reducing errors caused by missed transitions or unexpected screens
- Voice narration during recordings is now used to give the AI additional context about what was happening on screen

### Visual Feedback During Analysis
A scanning animation now plays while the AI analyzes recorded screenshots, so you can see that analysis is in progress.

### Reliability Improvements
- Fixed: Opening the Mobile Test Designer after it was previously closed could cause each AI-added step to appear twice
- Fixed: Claude AI assistant now starts correctly whether the app is launched from the Dock or from Terminal
