# Qaptly v0.17.8

A small follow-up to v0.17.7 focused on sign-in stability.

## No more surprise sign-outs

If Qaptly was left open for a while, the next action you took could sometimes drop you back to the login screen, or leave you stuck on a "session invalid" error until you fully signed out and back in. That whole class of issue is now handled behind the scenes — Qaptly quietly refreshes your session and continues what you were doing, so you stay signed in and your work isn't interrupted.

## Cleaner startup after tenant switches

When you launched Qaptly after having switched between tenants, the app could briefly get into a state where it thought you were signed in but couldn't reach any of your data. Startup now verifies your session against the tenant you're actually in and recovers automatically if anything is out of sync, so the app opens straight into a working state.
