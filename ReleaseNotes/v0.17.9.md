# Qaptly v0.17.9

Qaptly v0.17.9 makes test cases easier to reorganize, and makes screen capture on iPhone and iPad more dependable.

## Move test cases between folders

Test cases no longer have to stay where they were first created. From Test Case Manager you can move a single test case to another folder, or select several and move them all at once. Destination folders are shown with their full path, so you always know exactly where a case is going.

If some cases in a batch can't be moved, Qaptly tells you exactly how many succeeded and how many didn't, instead of leaving you to guess. After a move, the folder tree reloads with your open folders still open, and Qaptly checks with you first if you have unsaved edits that would be lost.

## More dependable iPhone and iPad screenshots

During manual testing on iOS, Qaptly can still capture screens through Xcode when a direct connection to the device isn't available — a capture route that had stopped being offered. When something isn't ready yet, the on-screen guidance now points you to what to check, and failures come with a clearer explanation.

## Other improvements

- The new move workflow is fully translated into English, Traditional Chinese, and Japanese.

Preview users can install this build to move back to the stable update channel.
